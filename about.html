Our Mission

Rediscovering the forgotten corners of cyberspace...

THE UNDERWEB exists as a portal to the forgotten realms of the internet, a digital archaeologist's dream space where the web's hidden dimensions converge. Our mission is to catalog and preserve the web's most ethereal, unusual, and overlooked corners.

In an age of algorithm-driven homogeneity, we celebrate the strange, the personal, and the experimental. We believe the internet should remain a place of dreams and discovery, not merely a platform for consumption and surveillance.

Join The Dig

Found a strange, personal or experimental corner of the web? Send it down. Links go into the directory and images go into the transmissions feed and the embeddable widget. A curator reviews each one first.

You can also take a button or the widget from the surface page and put it on your own site. The old web stayed alive because people pointed at each other.

Philosophy

We believe the internet was always meant to be a tapestry of individual expression rather than a monolithic marketplace. By seeking out the peculiar, the personal and the inexplicable corners of the web, we keep a connection to the internet's original promise: a place where every user is both explorer and creator.

Contact

For anything that isn't a submission, such as a broken link, a correction, or a request to remove your site, open an issue on GitHub. That's the one channel that gets read.
